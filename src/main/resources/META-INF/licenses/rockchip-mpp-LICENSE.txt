Rockchip MPP (Media Process Platform, https://github.com/rockchip-linux/mpp) provides the Rockchip hardware
video codecs used by FFmpeg's rkmpp decoders and encoders. It is linked statically into libavcodec.so.63,
only in the Linux ARM64 archive, built from the MPP 1.1.0 release that the JavaCPP preset recipe downloads.
